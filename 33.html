AlignAtt4LLM: Fast AlignAtt for Decoder-Only LLMs in an IWSLT 2026 Simultaneous Speech Translation System

Quentin Fuxa and Dominik Macháček

(Submission #33)

Summary

We present AlignAtt4LLM, an IWSLT 2026 simultaneous speech translation system for English-to-German, English-to-Italian, and English-to-Chinese. The system is a synchronous cascade combining Qwen3-ASR with forced alignment and Gemma-4 E4B-it MT under an AlignAtt policy adapted to decoder-only LLMs.

The main technical contribution is a practical decoder-only realization of AlignAtt: the source transcript is exposed as an explicit prompt span, translation-specific self-attention heads are selected offline, and a selective qk-fast replay reconstructs only the draft-to-source attention block needed by the online policy. The deployed runtime captures the exact query/key tensors used by vLLM while preserving model outputs.

On the IWSLT 2026 development set, the system reaches low-latency operating points near 2 seconds CU-LongYAAL and higher-quality regimes below 4 seconds, improving over the supplied no-context baselines for EN-DE and EN-IT while showing mixed results for EN-ZH.
