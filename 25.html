MLLP-VRAIN UPV system for the IWSLT 2026 Simultaneous Speech Translation task

Jorge Iranzo-Sánchez, Gerard Mas-Mollà, Adrià Gimenez, Jorge Civera Saiz, Albert Sanchis and Alfons Juan

(Submission #25)

Summary

This paper represents MLLP-VRAIN team's submission to the IWSLT 2026 Simultaneous Speech Translation track. We used a cascaded system built on Parakeet model and Qwen 3.5, enhanced with adaptive black-box policies and their relaxed variants to balance translation quality and latency in long-form SimulST.

We expand participation to all language directions and introduce a context-aware approach (for En→De/It/Zh) combining ASR word boosting with retrieval-augmented generation using pre-translated examples.

Results show a strong improvement over last year, achieving +5.82 XCOMET-XL on En→De, with an additional +1.03 gain from the context track, alongside a detailed latency analysis.
